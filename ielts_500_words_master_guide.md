# Part 1: Words 1 to 250 (Letters A to L)

## Letters A–B (Words 1–55)

| # | Word & POS | Tamil Meaning & Intuition | Academic Definition | Band 9 Collocation | Exemplar IELTS Academic Sentence |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Aberration** *(n)* | வழமைக்கு மாறான பிறழ்வு | A departure from what is normal or expected | *Statistical aberration* | *The sudden decline in graduation rates was treated as an aberration rather than a systemic policy failure.* |
| **2** | **Abate** *(v)* | தீவிரம் குறைதல் | To become less intense or widespread | *Storm abates / pollution abates* | *Only when storm surges abated could relief organizations distribute potable water to stranded villagers.* |
| **3** | **Abstain** *(v)* | விலகி இருத்தல் | To choose not to do or have something | *Abstain from voting / alcohol* | *Physicians strongly advise patients with liver disease to abstain entirely from alcoholic beverages.* |
| **4** | **Acclimate** *(v)* | புதிய சூழலுக்கு பழகுதல் | To become accustomed to a new climate or environment | *Acclimate to conditions* | *International students require several months to acclimate to freezing Scandinavian winter temperatures.* |
| **5** | **Accolade** *(n)* | பாராட்டு / விருது | An award or privilege granted as an honor | *Receive accolades* | *The pediatric surgeon earned international accolades for developing minimally invasive cardiac procedures.* |
| **6** | **Accumulate** *(v)* | சிறுக சிறுக சேர்த்தல் | To gather or acquire an increasing quantity of | *Accumulate wealth / data* | *Without financial literacy, young adults risk accumulating exorbitant credit card debt early in their careers.* |
| **7** | **Acquiesce** *(v)* | அரைமனதுடன் சம்மதித்தல் | To accept something reluctantly but without protest | *Acquiesce to demands* | *The municipal council ultimately acquiesced to environmental protestors' demands to halt logging.* |
| **8** | **Acrimonious** *(adj)* | கசப்புணர்வு நிறைந்த | Angry and bitter in tone | *Acrimonious debate* | *The acrimonious trade dispute between the neighboring states disrupted cross-border logistics for months.* |
| **9** | **Adaptable** *(adj)* | சூழலுக்கேற்ப மாறும் தன்மை | Able to adjust to new conditions readily | *Highly adaptable* | *In an automated global job market, adaptable graduates thrive far better than those tied to narrow vocations.* |
| **10** | **Adept** *(adj)* | கைதேர்ந்த / வல்லுநர் | Very skilled or proficient at something | *Adept at negotiations* | *Senior diplomats must be adept at defusing geopolitical hostility through empathetic communication.* |
| **11** | **Adhere** *(v)* | கொள்கையில் உறுதியாய் இருத்தல் | To stick firmly or believe in and follow practices | *Adhere to guidelines* | *Architectural engineers must adhere strictly to earthquake-resistant building codes in seismic zones.* |
| **12** | **Admonish** *(v)* | கண்டித்தல் / எச்சரித்தல் | To warn or reprimand someone firmly | *Admonish sternly* | *The judge admonished corporate executives for withholding safety audit findings from regulatory inspectors.* |
| **13** | **Adverse** *(adj)* | சாதகமற்ற / விரோதமான | Preventing success or development; harmful | *Adverse consequences* | *Prolonged exposure to fine particulate pollution produces severe adverse impacts on respiratory health.* |
| **14** | **Advocate** *(v/n)* | ஆதரித்துப் பேசுபவர் | To publicly recommend or support; a supporter | *Staunch advocate* | *Nelson Mandela remained an uncompromising advocate for universal democratic voting rights.* |
| **15** | **Aesthetic** *(adj)* | அழகுணர்வு சார்ந்த | Concerned with beauty or artistic appreciation | *Aesthetic appeal* | *Modern civic architecture must balance industrial functionality with timeless visual aesthetic harmony.* |
| **16** | **Affluent** *(adj)* | செல்வம் கொழிக்கும் | Having a great deal of money; wealthy | *Affluent suburbs* | *Families residing in affluent neighborhoods often enjoy disproportionate access to state-of-the-art public parks.* |
| **17** | **Aggravate** *(v)* | நிலைமையை மோசமாக்குதல் | To make a problem or injury worse or more serious | *Aggravate the condition* | *Consuming spicy, acidic food only aggravates chronic stomach ulcer inflammation.* |
| **18** | **Alleviate** *(v)* | வலியை / சுமையை குறைத்தல் | To make suffering, deficiency, or a problem less severe | *Alleviate poverty* | *Universal basic income pilots were instituted to alleviate extreme food insecurity among destitute households.* |
| **19** | **Allocate** *(v)* | நிதி அல்லது வளங்களை ஒதுக்குதல் | To distribute resources or duties for a specific purpose | *Allocate funds* | *The central treasury allocated 4% of gross domestic product directly to rural primary healthcare.* |
| **20** | **Allure** *(n)* | வசீகரிக்கும் கவர்ச்சி | The quality of being powerfully attractive or fascinating | *Irresistible allure* | *The economic allure of lucrative Silicon Valley salaries continues to attract world-class computer engineers.* |
| **21** | **Altruistic** *(adj)* | சுயநலமற்ற பெருந்தன்மை | Showing unselfish concern for others' welfare | *Altruistic motive* | *Organ donation represents a purely altruistic gesture that bestows the gift of life upon total strangers.* |
| **22** | **Ambiguous** *(adj)* | தெளிவற்ற / இருபொருள் தரும் | Open to more than one interpretation; unclear | *Ambiguous wording* | *Ambiguous contracts inevitably precipitate protracted legal disputes between corporate contractors.* |
| **23** | **Ambivalent** *(adj)* | முடிவெடுக்க முடியாத மன ஊசலாட்டம் | Having mixed feelings or contradictory ideas | *Ambivalent attitude* | *Citizens remain ambivalent regarding nuclear power, weighing its zero emissions against radioactive waste risks.* |
| **24** | **Ameliorate** *(v)* | நிலையை சிறப்பாக்குதல் | To make something bad or unsatisfactory better | *Ameliorate living conditions* | *Subsidized public transit schemes significantly ameliorate daily commuting stress for suburban workers.* |
| **25** | **Amenable** *(adj)* | இணங்கிப் போகும் மனநிலை | Open and responsive to suggestion; easily persuaded | *Amenable to compromise* | *Both labor union representatives were amenable to adopting flexible hybrid working schedules.* |
| **26** | **Amiable** *(adj)* | கனிவான / சிநேகபாவமுள்ள | Having or displaying a friendly and pleasant manner | *Amiable disposition* | *Her amiable nature allowed her to bridge misunderstandings across diverse multicultural project teams.* |
| **27** | **Anachronism** *(n)* | காலப்பொருத்தமற்ற பழமை | A thing belonging or appropriate to a period other than that in which it exists | *Historical anachronism* | *Enforcing dress codes that ban women from trousers in corporate settings is an offensive anachronism.* |
| **28** | **Analogous** *(adj)* | ஒப்பீடு செய்யத்தக்க | Comparable in certain respects | *Analogous situation* | *The functioning of a central processing unit (CPU) is roughly analogous to the human nervous system.* |
| **29** | **Anomaly** *(n)* | வழக்கத்திற்கு மாறான முரண்பாடு | Something that deviates from what is standard, normal, or expected | *Statistical anomaly* | *Climatologists confirmed that the sudden Arctic heatwave was not an anomaly, but a clear global warming trend.* |
| **30** | **Antecedent** *(n)* | முந்தைய முன்னோடி நிகழ்வு | A thing or event that existed before or logically precedes another | *Historical antecedent* | *The League of Nations served as the flawed diplomatic antecedent to the modern United Nations.* |
| **31** | **Anticipate** *(v)* | முன்கூட்டியே கணித்தல் | To regard as probable; expect or predict | *Anticipate demand* | *Urban planners must anticipate population surges when designing sewage and freshwater pipelines.* |
| **32** | **Antipathy** *(n)* | ஆழ்ந்த வெறுப்புணர்வு | A deep-seated feeling of dislike or aversion | *Deep-seated antipathy* | *The long-standing antipathy between the two rival political dynasties prevented meaningful parliamentary reform.* |
| **33** | **Antithesis** *(n)* | நேரெதிர் முரண் | A person or thing that is the direct opposite of someone or something else | *Exact antithesis* | *Unchecked commercial materialism is the exact antithesis of spiritual contentment and mental peace.* |
| **34** | **Apathy** *(n)* | அக்கறையின்மை / அலட்சியம் | Lack of interest, enthusiasm, or emotional concern | *Voter apathy* | *Pervasive voter apathy among young demographics undermines the foundational vitality of democratic governance.* |
| **35** | **Apex** *(n)* | உச்சக்கட்டம் / சிகரம் | The highest point or most successful culmination | *Apex of one's career* | *Winning the Nobel Prize in Physics represented the absolute apex of the professor's academic career.* |
| **36** | **Apprehensive** *(adj)* | எதிர்கால பயமும் கவலையும் | Anxious or fearful that something bad or unpleasant will happen | *Deeply apprehensive* | *First-time homebuyers are apprehensive about taking on massive mortgages during periods of interest rate volatility.* |
| **37** | **Arbitrary** *(adj)* | தன்னிச்சையான / காரணமற்ற | Based on random choice or personal whim rather than reason | *Arbitrary decision* | *Locking down entire neighborhoods without published health criteria was condemned as an arbitrary decree.* |
| **38** | **Archaic** *(adj)* | மிகப்பழைய / வழக்கொழிந்த | Very old or old-fashioned; outdated | *Archaic laws* | *Many developing countries still struggle under archaic colonial property statutes that disadvantage women.* |
| **39** | **Arduous** *(adj)* | கடினமான உழைப்பு கோரும் | Involving or requiring strenuous effort; difficult and tiring | *Arduous task* | *Trekking across the Himalayan mountain passes in deep winter remains an arduous, life-threatening feat.* |
| **40** | **Articulate** *(v/adj)* | தெளிவாக எடுத்துரைக்கும் திறன் | Fluent and clear in speech; to express an idea clearly | *Articulate speaker* | *Candidates who can articulate complex socio-economic arguments naturally achieve Band 8.5 in IELTS Speaking.* |
| **41** | **Ascertain** *(v)* | ஆராய்ந்து உறுதி செய்தல் | To find something out for certain; make sure of | *Ascertain the truth* | *Detectives conducted forensic ballistics tests to ascertain the exact trajectory of the fatal projectile.* |
| **42** | **Aspire** *(v)* | உயர்ந்த லட்சியம் கொள்ளுதல் | To direct one's hopes or ambitions toward achieving something | *Aspire to greatness* | *Many impoverished rural students aspire to become civil servants to eradicate bureaucratic corruption.* |
| **43** | **Assert** *(v)* | உறுதியாக எடுத்துரைத்தல் | To state a fact or belief confidently and forcefully | *Assert one's rights* | *Labor attorneys asserted that workers are constitutionally entitled to overtime compensation.* |
| **44** | **Assimilation** *(n)* | கலாச்சாரத்தில் ஒன்றிப்போதல் | The absorption and integration of people into a wider society | *Cultural assimilation* | *Successful assimilation requires host nations to combat xenophobia while immigrants learn official languages.* |
| **45** | **Astute** *(adj)* | சாதுரியமான கூர்மை அறிவு | Having or showing an ability to accurately assess situations to one's advantage | *Astute investor* | *The astute portfolio manager liquidated commercial real estate assets months before the market crash.* |
| **46** | **Attain** *(v)* | இலக்கை அடைதல் | To succeed in achieving or reaching something after effort | *Attain proficiency* | *Consistent daily reading of high-level journals is mandatory to attain Band 9 lexical fluency.* |
| **47** | **Augment** *(v)* | மதிப்பையோ அளவையோ பெருக்குதல் | To make something greater by adding to it; increase | *Augment income* | *Many schoolteachers take on evening freelance tutoring to augment their modest government salaries.* |
| **48** | **Austere** *(adj)* | மிக எளிமையான / கடுமையான | Severe or strict in manner, attitude, or appearance; plain | *Austere lifestyle* | *Buddhist monks maintain an austere lifestyle devoid of personal luxury, owning only simple robes and a bowl.* |
| **49** | **Autonomous** *(adj)* | தன்னாட்சி அதிகாரம் கொண்ட | Acting independently or having the freedom to govern itself | *Autonomous region* | *Granting autonomous administrative powers to tribal councils helped resolve decades of regional insurgent conflict.* |
| **50** | **Aversion** *(n)* | தீவிர வெறுப்பு / தவிர்த்தல் | A strong dislike or disinclination toward something | *Strong aversion* | *Her deep-seated aversion to public speaking made her reluctant to accept corporate management promotions.* |
| **51** | **Banal** *(adj)* | சாதாரணமான / சுவாரசியமற்ற | So lacking in originality as to be obvious and boring | *Banal commentary* | *The political debate deteriorated into a exchange of banal slogans lacking substantive policy solutions.* |
| **52** | **Barren** *(adj)* | தரிசு நிலம் / பலனற்ற | Too poor to produce much or any vegetation; unproductive | *Barren landscape* | *Centuries of overgrazing transformed once-lush Mediterranean hillsides into dry, barren wastelands.* |
| **53** | **Benevolent** *(adj)* | இரக்கமும் கருணையும் நிறைந்த | Well-meaning and kindly; serving a charitable purpose | *Benevolent ruler* | *The benevolent industrialist bequeathed his multibillion-dollar fortune to fund pediatric cancer research.* |
| **54** | **Bolster** *(v)* | கூடுதல் பலம் சேர்த்து தாங்குதல் | To support, strengthen, or prop up | *Bolster the economy* | *Central banks slashed benchmark interest rates to bolster commercial consumer spending during the downturn.* |
| **55** | **Burgeoning** *(adj)* | மளமளவென மலர்ந்து பெருகும் | Beginning to grow or increase rapidly; flourishing | *Burgeoning industry* | *Renewable solar energy has emerged as a burgeoning economic sector, generating hundreds of thousands of green jobs.* |

| **69** | **Cognitive** *(adj)* | சிந்தனை மற்றும் அறிவு சார்ந்த | Relating to mental processes of understanding and reasoning | *Cognitive abilities* | *Excessive early childhood screen time severely retards cognitive speech development in toddlers.* |
| **70** | **Coherent** *(adj)* | தர்க்கரீதியாக கோர்வையான | Logical, consistent, and easily understood | *Coherent argument* | *Candidates must present a coherent argumentative structure to achieve high band scores in IELTS Writing.* |

| **72** | **Collaborate** *(v)* | இணைந்து கூட்டுப்பணியாற்றுதல் | To work jointly on an activity or project | *Collaborate closely* | *Epidemiologists from twelve nations collaborated tirelessly to map the viral genetic sequence.* |
| **73** | **Commendable** *(adj)* | பாராட்டத்தக்க நற்செயல் | Deserving praise and approval | *Commendable effort* | *The municipality made commendable efforts to rescue homeless citizens during the unprecedented winter freeze.* |
| **74** | **Commensurate** *(adj)* | விகிதாசாரப்படி சமமான | Corresponding in size, degree, or proportion | *Commensurate with experience* | *Executive salaries should be commensurate with verifiable productivity rather than personal cronyism.* |

| **76** | **Compatible** *(adj)* | ஒன்றுக்கொன்று ஒத்துப் போகிற | Able to exist or occur together without conflict | *Fully compatible* | *Economic development must be environmentally compatible with the preservation of natural rainforests.* |
| **77** | **Compelling** *(adj)* | மறுக்க முடியாத வலிமை கொண்ட | Evoking interest, attention, or admiration powerfully | *Compelling evidence* | *The prosecution presented compelling DNA evidence linking the suspect conclusively to the crime scene.* |
| **78** | **Compensate** *(v)* | இழப்பீடு வழங்குதல் | To give someone something, typically money, in recognition of loss | *Compensate victims* | *The airline was mandated to compensate stranded passengers for luggage lost during the airport strike.* |

# 🎓 How to Master the 500 IELTS Words for Band 8.5–9.0

1. **Active Collocation Discipline**: Never memorize isolated words. Learn them in pairs:
   - Don't just learn *disparity* $\to$ Learn: **"glaring economic disparity"**
   - Don't just learn *exacerbate* $\to$ Learn: **"exacerbate the environmental crisis"**
   - Don't just learn *mitigate* $\to$ Learn: **"mitigate the catastrophic impacts"**

2. **Spelling Precision (Zero-Error Rule)**: In IELTS Writing and Listening, misspelling a word marks it wrong! Watch tricky double letters:
   - *Accommodate* (double 'c', double 'm')
   - *Embarrass* (double 'r', double 's')
   - *Indispensable* (ends in **-able**, not **-ible**)
   - *Maintenance* (spelled **main-ten-ance**, not **maintainance**)

3. **Contextual Naturalness**: Avoid "thesaurus stuffing." Use advanced words only when they express an exact, sophisticated academic nuance that simpler words cannot capture.
