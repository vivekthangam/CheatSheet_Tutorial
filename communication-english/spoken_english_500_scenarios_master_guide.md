# PART I: DAILY LIFE, TRAVEL & HEALTHCARE SCENARIOS (1–200)

## Category 1: Hotels, Stays & Hospitality (Scenarios 1–35)

#### Scenario 1: Checking In with an Online Booking
- **Context**: You arrive at the hotel reception in Bangalore or abroad with a MakeMyTrip/Agoda booking.
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I have booking. Name is Vivek. Room give."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, good afternoon! I have a reservation under the name Vivek Thangam. Here is my booking confirmation and government ID."*
- 💡 **Tamil Nuance**: Don't say "Name is...". Always say *"I have a reservation under the name..."*.

#### Scenario 2: Requesting an Early Check-In
- **Context**: You arrived at 10:00 AM, but standard check-in is at 2:00 PM (*Seekiram room kedaikkuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I came early only. Why room not ready? Give fast."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"I know standard check-in is at 2 PM, but I've just arrived from an early flight. Would an early check-in be possible if a room is already cleaned?"*
- 💡 **Tamil Nuance**: "Would... be possible" softens the request so you don't sound demanding.

#### Scenario 3: Requesting a Quiet or High-Floor Room
- **Context**: You want a room away from street traffic or lift noise (*Satham illadha room venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"No sound room give. Top floor keep."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"If available, could I please have a room on a higher floor away from the elevator? I have important work calls today."*

#### Scenario 4: Asking for the Wi-Fi Credentials
- **Context**: You need hotel Wi-Fi for your laptop (*Wi-Fi password enna?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Wi-Fi password what? Net not coming."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please share the Wi-Fi network name and password? Is there a login portal?"*

#### Scenario 5: Keycard Demagnetized / Not Opening Door
- **Context**: The room card flashes red at the door (*Card velai seiyala*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Card is dead. Door not opening."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, it seems my keycard has been demagnetized. It's flashing red at room 402. Could you please reactivate it for me?"*

#### Scenario 6: Air Conditioner (AC) Remote / Cooling Not Working
- **Context**: The AC in your room is blowing warm air (*AC cool aagala*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"AC is not cooling. Change room or repair fast."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Good evening, this is room 310. The air conditioner isn't cooling properly; it's blowing warm air. Could you please send maintenance up to check it?"*

#### Scenario 7: Ordering Room Service Dinner
- **Context**: Calling the hotel kitchen to order dinner (*Room service-la saapadu order pannanum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Send 2 rotis and paneer butter masala to room 205."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, I'd like to place an order for in-room dining, please. Could I have two butter naans and one paneer butter masala? Also, could you let me know the approximate delivery time?"*

#### Scenario 8: Requesting Extra Towels and Toiletries
- **Context**: You need extra bath towels and dental kit (*Extra towel venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give two towels and paste to my room."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, could housekeeping please send up two extra bath towels and a dental kit to room 512?"*

#### Scenario 9: Requesting an Iron Box and Ironing Board
- **Context**: Your formal shirt is wrinkled for an interview/meeting (*Iron box venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Need iron box immediately."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please arrange an iron and an ironing board in my room?"*

#### Scenario 10: Asking About Breakfast Timing and Location
- **Context**: Checking where the complimentary buffet is served (*Breakfast enga, eppo?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Morning food where? What time starts?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you tell me what time breakfast is served tomorrow morning, and which restaurant it's located in?"*

#### Scenario 11: Requesting a Wake-Up Call
- **Context**: You have an early morning flight (*Kaalaila 5 manikku call panni ezhupunga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give wake-up call at 5 AM without fail."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could I please request a wake-up call tomorrow morning at 5:00 AM for room 408?"*

#### Scenario 12: Inquiring About Laundry Service Rates
- **Context**: You want trousers dry-cleaned (*Thuni thovikka kudukkanum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I have dress for wash. How much money?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Do you offer same-day laundry service, and could you let me know where I can find the price list and laundry bag?"*

#### Scenario 13: Water Leaking in the Bathroom
- **Context**: The flush tank or faucet is dripping constantly (*Bathroom-la thanni ottudhu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Bathroom water is falling. Come and see."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, this is Vivek in room 204. There is a continuous leak under the bathroom sink that's pooling on the floor. Could someone inspect it right away?"*

#### Scenario 14: Noisy Neighbors in the Next Room
- **Context**: Loud party or TV in adjacent room past midnight (*Pakkathu room-la romba satham*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Tell next room people to shut up."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi front desk, the guests in room 306 are playing very loud music and shouting. It's past midnight; could you please ask them to keep the noise down?"*

#### Scenario 15: Requesting a Late Check-Out
- **Context**: Your flight is at 8:00 PM, and checkout is 11:00 AM (*Late-ah checkout pannalama?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I will leave at 3 PM only. Okay?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"My flight is late this evening. Would it be possible to extend my check-out time until 2:00 PM? Please let me know if any additional charges apply."*

#### Scenario 16: Leaving Luggage with the Concierge After Check-Out
- **Context**: You checked out, but want to tour the city without bags (*Bag-ah inga vachutu pogalama?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Keep my bags here. I will take evening."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"I have checked out, but could I store my luggage with the concierge until 6:00 PM? Could I get the baggage claim tag?"*

#### Scenario 17: Requesting a Taxi to the Airport
- **Context**: Asking the front desk to arrange an airport cab (*Airport-ku cab book panna mudiyuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Book airport taxi for me at 4 PM."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please arrange a cab to the international airport tomorrow morning at 7:00 AM? What would the approximate fare be?"*

#### Scenario 18: Disputing an Unfamiliar Charge on the Final Bill
- **Context**: The bill includes a minibar charge you never used (*Naan edhuvum saapdala, edhukku charge?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Why this charge? I didn't drink anything! Remove it!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"I noticed a 600-rupee charge for minibar snacks on the bill. However, I didn't consume anything from the minibar. Could you please double-check that with housekeeping and remove the charge?"*

#### Scenario 19: Requesting a GST / Corporate Invoice
- **Context**: You need the bill under your employer's company name for reimbursement (*Company name-la bill venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give bill on company name with GST."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please generate the invoice with our company name and GST number for corporate reimbursement? Here are the company billing details."*

#### Scenario 20: Safe Locker Locked / Forgotten PIN
- **Context**: Digital safe inside the wardrobe won't open (*Safe locker open aagala*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Locker is stuck. Open it."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, my in-room digital safe isn't accepting the PIN code. Could you send the duty manager with the master key to help me unlock it?"*

#### Scenario 21: Requesting a Room Change Due to Bad Odor
- **Context**: The room smells like cigarette smoke in a non-smoking room (*Room-la smoke smell adikkudhu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Room is stinking. Give another."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, I booked a non-smoking room, but there is a strong cigarette odor here in room 405. Would it be possible to move to another room?"*

#### Scenario 22: Inquiring About Gym and Swimming Pool Hours
- **Context**: Finding out fitness center timings (*Gym eppo open?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Gym when opens? Pool free or cost?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you let me know the operating hours for the fitness center and the rooftop pool? Are there any dress code requirements?"*

#### Scenario 23: Asking for Mineral Water Refill
- **Context**: Both complimentary water bottles are finished (*Innum 2 bottle thanni venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Water over. Bring bottle."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, could housekeeping please send up two extra bottles of complimentary drinking water to room 108?"*

#### Scenario 24: Asking if Outside Food (Swiggy/Zomato) is Allowed
- **Context**: You ordered Biryani on Swiggy (*Swiggy delivery room-ku varuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Swiggy allowed? Delivery guy come up?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, does the hotel permit outside food delivery, or should I collect the order from the lobby security desk?"*

#### Scenario 25: Asking for an Adaptor / Plug Converter
- **Context**: Your Indian three-pin laptop charger doesn't fit the US socket (*Pin adapter irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Plug not fitting. Give converter."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Do you have an international travel adapter I could borrow for my laptop charger?"*

#### Scenario 26: Requesting an Extra Bed / Rollaway Cot
- **Context**: Three colleagues sharing a twin room (*Extra bed pooda mudiyuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Put one extra bed here."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we request a rollaway cot for this room? What is the additional charge per night?"*

#### Scenario 27: Asking for Local Sightseeing Recommendations
- **Context**: Asking the concierge what to visit in the city (*Pakkatula enna paarkalam?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Tell tourist places near hotel."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you recommend a few must-visit landmarks and reliable local restaurants within a 15-minute drive from the hotel?"*

#### Scenario 28: Lost Room Keycard Outside the Hotel
- **Context**: You lost the room key in a mall (*Card tholanju pochu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I lost card. Don't charge me."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Unfortunately, I seem to have misplaced my room keycard while I was out. Could you please issue a replacement card and deactivate the old one?"*

#### Scenario 29: Inquiring About Airport Shuttle Service
- **Context**: Checking if the hotel has a free airport van (*Free airport bus irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Hotel bus there for airport?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Does the hotel provide a complimentary shuttle service to the airport, or should we arrange a private cab?"*

#### Scenario 30: Asking for Hair Dryer
- **Context**: Bathrooms in some budget hotels don't keep it mounted (*Hair dryer venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Send hair dryer to dry hair."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could housekeeping please bring a hair dryer to room 215?"*

#### Scenario 31: TV Cable / Streaming App Not Working
- **Context**: TV displays "No Signal" (*TV-la signal varala*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"TV is not running. Channel no signal."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Good evening, the television in room 302 is displaying a 'No Signal' error. Could you have someone reset the set-top box?"*

#### Scenario 32: Extension of Stay for Another Night
- **Context**: Your client meeting got delayed by one more day (*Innum oru naal thanga mudiyuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I stay one more day. Don't checkout me."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, my client meeting has been extended. Is it possible to extend my reservation for one more night in the same room?"*

#### Scenario 33: Requesting a Blanket Due to Cold Air Conditioning
- **Context**: Central AC is too cold (*Romba kulirudhu, extra blanket venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Too much cold. Send thick bedsheet."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Hi, the central AC is quite chilly tonight. Could housekeeping send an extra heavy blanket to room 409?"*

#### Scenario 34: Asking for Currency Exchange (Forex)
- **Context**: Exchanging US Dollars to Indian Rupees or Euros (*Foreign currency mathanuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Change my dollars to rupees. How much rate?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Do you offer foreign currency exchange at the front desk, or is there an authorized money changer nearby?"*

#### Scenario 35: Praising Great Service During Checkout
- **Context**: You want to express appreciation to the front desk team (*Service romba nalla irundhuchu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Hotel was super, I liked."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"I wanted to thank you for a wonderful stay. The hospitality and room service were exceptional. We'll definitely recommend your hotel!"*

## Category 2: Restaurants, Cafes & Food Ordering (Scenarios 36–75)

#### Scenario 36: Reserving a Table Over the Phone
- **Context**: Booking a table for dinner for 4 people (*Dinner-ku 4 perukku table reserve pannanum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I want table for 4 members tonight at 8 PM. Keep seat."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Good afternoon! I'd like to reserve a table for four tonight at 8:00 PM under the name Vivek."*
- 💡 **Tamil Nuance**: Never say "four members". Always say *"a table for four"* or *"four people"*.

#### Scenario 37: Walking in Without a Reservation
- **Context**: Reaching a busy restaurant on a Saturday evening (*Reservation illa, table irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Booking not there. Any seat free?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Good evening! We don't have a reservation. Do you have a table available for two, or what is the approximate wait time?"*

#### Scenario 38: Requesting a Window or Outdoor Seat
- **Context**: Choosing a nice quiet spot (*Jannal pakkam table irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give window side table."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"If possible, could we get a table by the window or on the outdoor patio?"*

#### Scenario 39: Asking if Food is Pure Vegetarian / Vegan
- **Context**: You are a strict vegetarian traveling in Europe/US (*Idhula muttai / asaidham irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"I am pure veg. No non-veg. It has egg?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Excuse me, I'm a strict vegetarian. Does this soup contain any animal broth, meat, or eggs?"*

#### Scenario 40: Specifying Low Spice / Non-Spicy Level
- **Context**: You want food that isn't burning spicy (*Karam koraiva poodunga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Don't put mirchi. Very less spicy do."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please make the pasta mild? I have a low tolerance for spice, so minimal chili/pepper, please."*

#### Scenario 41: Asking About Allergen Information (Nut / Peanut Allergy)
- **Context**: You are allergic to nuts (*Enakku kadalai allergy*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"No groundnut! I will get allergy!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"I have a severe peanut allergy. Can you confirm if this dish is prepared with peanut oil or has cross-contamination with nuts?"*

#### Scenario 42: Ordering Water: Bottled vs. Regular Tap Water
- **Context**: Deciding what water to drink (*Normal thanni podhum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Normal water give."* (Abroad, this can cause confusion).
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we have a pitcher of tap water, please?"* (In US/UK) OR *"We'd prefer bottled mineral water, room temperature, please."* (In India/Asia).

#### Scenario 43: Asking for the Waiter's Recommendation
- **Context**: You don't know what is good on the menu (*Inga enna special?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"What is best here? Tell me to eat."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"What would you recommend for starters? What are your chef's specialties tonight?"*

#### Scenario 44: Calling the Waiter Politely
- **Context**: Getting the waiter's attention across the room (*Server-ah koobidanum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Hello! Boss! Bhaiyya! Brother! Server!"* (Snapping fingers or hissing *'Tss-tss'*).
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *(Make eye contact, raise your hand slightly, and say with a warm smile)*: *"Excuse me, sir?"* or *"Excuse me, when you have a moment?"*

#### Scenario 45: Ordering Starters First, Mains Later
- **Context**: You want starters right away, main course later (*First starter kondu vaanga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"First bring starter. Main food later bring."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Let's start with the garlic bread and mushroom soup first. We'll order the main course in a little while."*

#### Scenario 46: Asking for Extra Cutlery / Spoons and Forks
- **Context**: One colleague doesn't have a fork (*Innoru fork venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Fork missing. Bring one."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we please get an extra fork and a couple of napkins for this side of the table?"*

#### Scenario 47: Asking for Extra Water Refill
- **Context**: Your water glass is empty (*Thanni theerndhu pochu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Pour water fast."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could you please top up our glasses with some more water?"*

#### Scenario 48: Politely Pointing Out a Cold Dish
- **Context**: Soup or curry arrived lukewarm (*Saapadu aari pochu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Soup is ice. Reheat it."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Excuse me, the soup seems to be lukewarm. Would you mind heating it up a bit more, please?"*

#### Scenario 49: The Wrong Dish Was Delivered
- **Context**: You ordered paneer, but they brought chicken or pasta (*Naan idhai order pannala*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Why you brought this? I told paneer! Take it back!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Excuse me, I believe there's been a slight mix-up. I ordered the grilled paneer, but this looks like chicken. Could you please check with the kitchen?"*

#### Scenario 50: Finding a Hair or Foreign Object in Food
- **Context**: Unhygienic hair found in the plate (*Saapaatula mudi irukku*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"See! Hair is there! Very dirty hotel!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *(Call waiter discreetly)*: *"Excuse me, could you take a look here? Unfortunately, there's a hair inside this dish. Could you please replace it with a fresh plate?"*

#### Scenario 51: Asking to Pack Remaining Food (Parcel / To-Go)
- **Context**: Half the pizza is left over (*Michatha pack panna mudiyuma?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Parcel this food."* ("Parcel" is Indian English; Westerners say "to-go box" or "takeaway").
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"We couldn't finish everything. Could you please pack the remaining pizza in a to-go box?"*

#### Scenario 52: Asking for the Bill / Check
- **Context**: Ready to pay and leave (*Bill kondu vaanga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Bill please!"* / *"Account bring!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we get the check, please?"* (US) or *"Could we have the bill, please?"* (UK/India).

#### Scenario 53: Splitting the Bill Equally Among Four Friends
- **Context**: Four friends want to divide the total equally (*Ellarum equal-ah share panni pay panrom*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Make 4 parts of bill. Everyone will pay own."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we split the bill equally across four credit cards, please?"*

#### Scenario 54: Paying Separately for What Each Person Ate
- **Context**: You only ate a salad; friend ordered steak (*Naan saaptadhukku mattum pay panren*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Separate my food price."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Would it be possible to get separate checks, or should we calculate our individual items?"*

#### Scenario 55: Asking if Service Charge is Already Included
- **Context**: Checking if the bill already has tip included (*Tip idhula irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Tip added or not added?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Is the service charge already included in the total, or is gratuity separate?"*

#### Scenario 56: Asking to Pay via UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe)
- **Context**: Paying through QR code (*GPay scanner irukka?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"GPay scanner give."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Do you accept UPI or Google Pay? Could you bring the QR code scanner to the table?"*

#### Scenario 57: Card Declined at the Payment Terminal
- **Context**: POS machine shows "Transaction Failed" (*Card reject aayiduchu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Machine is faulty! My card has lot of balance!"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"It looks like this card didn't go through. Let me try my other card, or I can pay via UPI."*

#### Scenario 58: Asking for More Ice in a Beverage
- **Context**: Warm soda (*Extra ice cubes venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give ice cubes."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could I have a few extra ice cubes on the side, please?"*

#### Scenario 59: Ordering Coffee at Starbucks: Customization
- **Context**: Wanting oat milk and sugar-free vanilla syrup (*Oat milk-la cappuccino venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Give one cappuccino with oat milk, no sugar."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could I please get a medium cappuccino with oat milk and two pumps of sugar-free vanilla syrup?"*

#### Scenario 60: Asking for Dressing on the Side (Salad)
- **Context**: You don't want mayonnaise drenched all over your vegetables (*Sauce thaniya vachudunga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Don't mix sauce. Keep outside."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could I get the Caesar salad, but with the dressing on the side, please?"*

#### Scenario 61: Inquiring if Bread/Chips Refills are Free
- **Context**: Basket of complimentary bread is finished (*Innoru basket free-ah?*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Next bread free or cost?"*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Are complimentary refills included for the bread basket, or is there an extra charge?"*

#### Scenario 62: Requesting Kids' Cutlery or High Chair
- **Context**: Dining with a toddler (*Kozhandhaikku chair venum*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Baby chair give."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Could we please get a high chair for our toddler and some plastic cutlery?"*

#### Scenario 63: Telling the Waiter You're Still Deciding
- **Context**: Waiter comes to take order, but you haven't read menu yet (*Innum decide pannala, konjam time kudunga*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Go now, come later. Still looking."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"We're still browsing through the menu. Could you give us another five minutes, please?"*

#### Scenario 64: Praising the Chef for an Outstanding Meal
- **Context**: The Biryani or pasta was phenomenal (*Saapadu romba arumaiya irundhuchu*).
- ❌ **What NOT to Say**: *"Food is super taste. Tell cook."*
- ✅ **What TO Say**: *"Please convey our compliments to the chef! The mutton biryani was absolutely delicious."*

#### Scenario 65: Asking About Buffet vs. À la Carte
- **Context**: Wondering if you can order individual items instead of full buffet (*Buffet-ah illa a la carte-ah?*).

## 🎯 Final Words: The Golden Rule for Tamil Engineers

1. **Slow Down**: You do not need to speak at 200 words per minute. Global leaders speak at 120–140 words per minute with purposeful pauses.
2. **Eliminate the "Is-"**: Practice saying *Scale, School, State, Spring* without the "i" sound.
3. **Never Say "Doubt"**: Say *"I have a question"* or *"Could you clarify?"*.
4. **Speak with Value, Not Raw Code**: Frame technical achievements in terms of latency saved, customer bugs eliminated, and business impact.
